NEW EMPIRE - PLAYTEST BUILD
===========================

Thank you for playing. This is an early build of a real-time strategy
game in the spirit of the 1997 original. The art and every sound are
stand-ins; the game underneath is what we want you to play.

OPENING IT (once)

  The app is not notarised by Apple, so the first launch is refused.

  macOS 15 or later: double-click New Empire.app and click Done. Open
  System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the note that
  "New Empire" was blocked, and click Open Anyway. Confirm once more.

  Older macOS: Control-click New Empire.app and choose Open.

  Or, in Terminal, from the folder this file is in:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "New Empire.app"

  After that it opens like any other app. Apple Silicon Macs only.

PLAYING

  Play until you want to stop.

  NEW GAME, then START, gives you a match against one computer opponent.
  F1 shows every control. Right-click sends villagers to work and units
  to attack. There is no tutorial: the game should teach itself, and
  where it does not, that is exactly what we want to hear.

WHAT TO SEND BACK

  - What you thought, in your own words: what felt right, what was
    missing, what made you swear.
  - Every match is recorded automatically. The recordings are in
        ~/Library/Application Support/new-empire/replays/
    Send the file for any match you talk about and we can watch what
    you saw.
  - If anything goes wrong, what you did just before.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR MAC

  The game has no network code. Settings, saves and recordings live in
  ~/Library/Application Support/new-empire/ and nowhere else. Delete
  that folder to remove every trace.

FOR THE OBSERVER

  Fresh player, fresh settings: delete settings.ron from that folder
  before each session, so the first-time hints show. F4 opens the
  performance readout; read it during the biggest fight. The sheet is
  docs/09 section 9.1 in the repository.
